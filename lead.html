What changed on Lead Overview

  1. 1
    Overview is a summary. Before, it repeated every tab as a full working block. Now it shows summary cards that open their tab, where the real work happens. The numbers come from the tabs themselves.
  2. 2
    The AI chat looks like a chat. It is renamed to "Lead Assistant" and has its own colour and avatar, so it doesn't blend in with the data cards. Suggested questions and an input field show that you can ask about the lead.
  3. 3
    The column toggles have a label. The unlabelled filter icons are replaced by a "Columns" menu with the heading "Show columns in this table", so it's clear what they affect.
  4. 4
    Preview is separated from the column controls. It is now a labelled button, "Preview estimate (PDF)", set apart by a divider.
  5. 5
    Agenda actions are clear. The "Edit" column with five unlabelled icons becomes "Actions", with a ⋯ menu of named items: Edit, Reschedule, Reassign, Duplicate, Delete.
  6. 6
    Completing a task uses a checkbox. The warning icon is replaced by a "Done" checkbox at the start of each row. A done task is crossed out and its status changes to "Done".
  7. 7
    One primary action per area. "Send to archive" becomes a secondary button in the header. Green is kept for the main action in each block. The Agreement section is unchanged, as the client asked.
